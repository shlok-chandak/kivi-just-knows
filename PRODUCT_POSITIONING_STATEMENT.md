# PRODUCT POSITIONING STATEMENT

Kivi is a voice native agent that lives on your screen & works in every application you use.

Talk to Kivi naturally in your own language and it understands the context around you, be it the newsletter you are reading, the doc you are working on or the slack thread you are replying to.

Kivi puts an AI assistant across apps that you use daily. Use it to dictate messages, summarize articles, reframe texts, respond to emails, research, or get something done all without leaving the program you are using.

And because Kivi learns how you communicate, write, and work, it gets more personal with every interaction.

It’s the AI that works where you work.
